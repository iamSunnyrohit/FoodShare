## Brand & Style
The brand personality centers on warmth, community trust, ecological vitality, and wholesome accessibility. Designed for neighborhood food rescuers, local donors, volunteers, and nutrition-conscious individuals, the platform communicates care and reliability without feeling institutional or clinical.

The visual direction merges **Minimalism** with **Tactile, Organic Warmth**:
- Generous cream-toned negative space establishes an inviting, approachable atmosphere.
- Crisp, rounded surface cards keep content organized and scannable.
- Natural greens evoke sustainable agriculture and garden freshness, while warm citrus accents generate gentle urgency around perishable foods.
- Tactile affordances—such as subtle ambient elevation and pill-shaped interactive triggers—create an intuitive, welcoming touch interface across devices.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in earth and plant tones, anchored by clean light backgrounds to prioritize food photography and legibility.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#2F7D4F`)**: Vibrant garden green. Used for key call-to-actions, active filter states, verified indicators, and core brand accents.
- **Secondary / Deep Pine (`#14332A`)**: Deep forest tone. Reserved for dominant headlines, primary brand marks, navigation bars, and strong visual weight.
- **Accent / Alert (`#F2994A`)**: Warm sunlit amber. Used for expiring food countdowns, urgent pickups, nutrition spotlights, and critical notifications.
- **Neutral / Text Body (`#1F2A24`)**: Deep charcoal with a subtle forest cast, ensuring high legibility while harmonizing with the palette.
- **Muted Text (`#607268`)**: Secondary typography, captions, and non-critical metadata.
- **Background (`#FAF8F3`)**: Warm off-white parchment foundation that eliminates sterile digital glare.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white cards and sheets that lift off the warm foundation.
- **Primary Tint (`#E8F3EC`)**: Soft sage tint for badge backdrops, subtle tags, and active list item selections.
- **Border Outline (`#E4E1D8`)**: Warm stone tone for low-contrast structure, dividers, and card borders.

### Status Tones
- **Available (`#2F7D4F`)**: Fresh status paired with leaf iconography.
- **Claimed (`#E0A526`)**: Holding status paired with hand/receipt iconography.
- **In Transit (`#3B82C4`)**: Operational sky blue paired with transit iconography.
- **Delivered (`#14332A`)**: Fulfilled deep evergreen paired with checkmark iconography.
- **Cancelled (`#8A8F8B`)**: Inactive slate gray paired with dismissive iconography.

## Typography
The system uses a geometric, warm display typeface (`Epilogue`) for titles and brand expression, supported by `Inter` for exceptional UI density, legibility, and tabular clarity across recipe details and claim logs.

- **Headlines (`Epilogue`, SemiBold 600)**: Deliver character and community warmth while maintaining strong structure. Always set in deep pine (`#14332A`) to ensure authoritative reading flow.
- **Body (`Inter`, Regular 400 & Medium 500)**: Provides high readability for ingredients, pickup instructions, and volunteer logs. Rendered in deep charcoal green (`#1F2A24`).
- **Labels & Microcopy (`Inter`, Medium 500)**: Used across buttons, badges, count tags, and dietary metadata.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on an 8-point spatial model mapped directly to clean fluid containers.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max-width container capped at 1280px. Outer canvas margin is 32px (`2rem`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px page margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px page margins.

### Spacing Application
- Component internal padding strictly utilizes `space-xs` (8px), `space-sm` (12px), `space-md` (16px), and `space-lg` (24px).
- Inter-card and structural grid gaps scale from `16px` on mobile viewports up to `24px` on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth follows an airy, clean, low-shadow strategy designed to keep food photography front and center.

- **Base Surfaces**: Cards, modals, and drawers sit on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) against the `#FAF8F3` backdrop.
- **Ambient Shadow**: Elevated cards leverage an organic, low-opacity shadow tinted with the deep forest green rather than synthetic gray:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(20, 51, 42, 0.05);`
- **Interactive Hover Depth**: On interactive cards and listings, hover transitions subtly lift the element:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 28px rgba(20, 51, 42, 0.08); transform: translateY(-2px);`
- **Borders & Dividers**: All elevated modules feature a 1px border (`#E4E1D8`) to ensure crisp definition on screens with wide color gamuts.

## Shapes
The shape language combines gentle, friendly modular cards with pill-shaped interactive targets.

- **Surfaces & Cards**: `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) applied to content cards, modal sheets, filter drawers, and nutrition information tiles.
- **Buttons, Badges & Pills**: `rounded-full` (9999px) applied to action triggers, category chips, status pills, and search inputs to encourage friendly, tactile interaction.
- **Thumbnails & Avatars**: Food preview images carry an interior 12px border radius when nested within cards; contributor profile images remain fully circular (pill/avatar).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill (`9999px`), `#2F7D4F` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#266942` with subtle 2px upward lift.
- **Secondary**: Full pill, `#E8F3EC` tint background with `#2F7D4F` text. Hover shifts to `#D8EADE`.
- **Urgent / Accent**: Full pill, `#F2994A` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Used exclusively for imminent expiry food claims and high-priority alerts.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent surface with `#14332A` text, underlined on hover or framed with a 1px `#E4E1D8` border.

### Status Pills & Chips
- **Chips**: Pill radius, height 32px, padded with 12px horizontally. Neutral chips feature a `#FFFFFF` base, 1px `#E4E1D8` outline, and `#1F2A24` text. Active chips take `#E8F3EC` fill with `#2F7D4F` text and border.
- **Status Badges**: Semi-transparent or tinted backgrounds matched to their operational state:
  - *Available*: `#E8F3EC` background, `#2F7D4F` text and icon.
  - *Claimed*: `#FCF4E0` background, `#E0A526` text and icon.
  - *In Transit*: `#EBF3FA` background, `#3B82C4` text and icon.
  - *Delivered*: `#E7ECE9` background, `#14332A` text and icon.
  - *Cancelled*: `#F1F2F1` background, `#8A8F8B` text and icon.

### Cards
- **Food Listing Card**: Built on a `#FFFFFF` base, 16px radius, with a 1px `#E4E1D8` border and subtle ambient shadow. Features an edge-to-edge or inset image with 12px radii, status badge positioned at the top-left, remaining countdown badge on the top-right, and an actionable bottom row containing dietary tags (e.g., Vegan, Gluten-Free, Halal).

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Search & Text Inputs**: Minimum height 48px, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E4E1D8` outline, 9999px (search) or 12px (text area) radius. Placeholder text in `#607268`. Focus transitions to a 2px `#2F7D4F` ring with zero shadow glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Rounded (4px for checkbox, full pill for radio), `#FFFFFF` base with 1.5px `#E4E1D8` border. Checked states fill with `#2F7D4F` showing a crisp white glyph.

### Nutrition & Impact Meters
- **Metrics Tiles**: Light cream or sage containers detailing carbon offset, meals saved, and caloric or macro distribution. Uses `#14332A` for values and `#607268` for sub-labels.
- **Progress Bars**: 8px height, rounded full. Inactive track is `#E8F3EC`, progress indicator fills with `#2F7D4F` or `#F2994A` depending on critical capacity.
